Music

Music is a beautiful form of art that expresses emotions, feelings, and stories through sound. It is enjoyed by people all around the world and comes in many different forms, such as classical, Bollywood, Western, and instrumental music. Music can make us feel happy, relaxed, energetic, or emotional. It also brings people together and is an important part of many cultures and celebrations.
