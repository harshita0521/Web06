Seven Sisters of India

Arunachal Pradesh

Capital: Itanagar

Known for its mountains, monasteries and beautiful valleys.

Assam

Capital: Dispur

Famous for tea gardens, wildlife and the Brahmaputra River.

Meghalaya

Capital: Shillong

Known for waterfalls, caves and living root bridges.

Manipur

Capital: Imphal

Known for Loktak Lake and its rich cultural traditions.

Mizoram

Capital: Aizawl

Known for green hills, forests and scenic landscapes.

Nagaland

Capital: Kohima

Known for its diverse tribal cultures and Hornbill Festival.

Tripura

Capital: Agartala

Known for its palaces, temples and natural beauty.
