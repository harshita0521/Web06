Wildlife

Wildlife includes animals, birds and other living creatures found in forests, oceans, grasslands and other natural habitats.

Mammals

Mammals are animals that usually have hair or fur and feed their young with milk. Tigers, elephants and lions are examples.

Birds

Birds are found in almost every part of the world. Eagles, parrots, peacocks and owls are some examples.

Reptiles

Reptiles are cold-blooded animals such as snakes, crocodiles, lizards and turtles.

Marine Life

Marine life includes animals and plants that live in oceans and seas, such as whales, dolphins, sharks and sea turtles.
